# Non-Functional Requirements Catalogue

| ID | Quality attribute | Draft requirement | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|
| NFR-01 | Security | Access shall be restricted by role and business authorization. | Role-based access tests |
| NFR-02 | Auditability | Key actions shall be logged with actor and timestamp. | Audit evidence review |
| NFR-03 | Privacy | Only data necessary for the process shall be collected and shown to each role. | Privacy review |
| NFR-04 | Availability | Availability target shall be agreed with the business owner. | Monitoring and service review |
| NFR-05 | Performance | Response-time targets shall be defined after usage and volume are understood. | Performance test |
| NFR-06 | Recovery | Backup and recovery objectives shall be documented and tested. | Recovery exercise |
| NFR-07 | Usability | Form labels, validation messages, and status names shall be understandable to target users. | Usability test |

Avoid inventing numerical service levels before stakeholder and technical discovery.
