# Business Problem Statement

## Background
A fictional mid-sized manufacturing company receives internal purchase requests from multiple departments. Requests are submitted through email and spreadsheets. Procurement staff manually check completeness and coordinate approvals through separate email conversations.

## Problem
The current process creates inconsistent request data, repeated clarification, limited status visibility, and fragmented approval records.

## Business impact (hypotheses to validate)
- Longer request cycle time due to incomplete submissions and repeated follow-up.
- Time spent by procurement staff answering status questions.
- Difficulty identifying bottlenecks and overdue approvals.
- Reduced auditability because decision records are distributed across messages.

No quantitative baseline is assumed. Cycle time, rework rate, pending-request age, and first-pass completeness should be measured before claiming improvement.

## Desired outcomes
1. Standardized request intake.
2. Clear validation before submission.
3. Traceable approval status.
4. Consistent audit history.
5. Operational dashboard for pending requests and bottlenecks.

## Key discovery questions
- How many requests are submitted each month?
- What percentage are returned for correction?
- What are the approval roles and thresholds?
- What is the current average cycle time?
- Which ERP or finance system stores supplier and cost-center data?
- What retention and access policies apply?
